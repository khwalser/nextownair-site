Itinerary mode

Choose when to move—and when to linger.

Each place you choose is a stop. NexTownAir starts destination stops at two nights so the trip feels like a journey, while airports inserted automatically as connectors stay quick by default. You can change any stop to later today, 1–4 nights, or an exact departure date.
